The letters you carry.

A name number, often called an expression number, is made by assigning a value to each letter in a name. In this symbolic tradition, the interpretation offers language for reflection on expression and personal style. It does not measure your abilities.

The Pythagorean letter chart

Each A–Z letter has one repeating value from 1 to 9.

Example: Jane Doe

J = 1 · A = 1 · N = 5 · E = 5

D = 4 · O = 6 · E = 5

1 + 1 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 6 + 5 = 27 → 9

Name number: 9 / The Humanitarian

Which name should I enter?

Traditionally, use the full name at birth, including middle names. You can also explore a chosen name, married name or nickname as a separate comparison. Different spellings can produce different results; this is a consequence of the mapping, not evidence that a name makes someone lucky or unlucky.

How names are normalized

Case, spaces, apostrophes, hyphens and periods do not affect the total. Common Latin accents are removed, so José becomes JOSE. Æ becomes AE, Œ becomes OE, Ø becomes O, Ł becomes L, Ð and Đ become D, Þ becomes TH, and ß becomes SS. The exact letters used appear in your calculation.

This is an A–Z system. Numbers, emoji and unsupported characters are rejected rather than silently counted. If your name uses another writing system, choose a consistent Latin transliteration first. Different transliterations may give different results, and no universally correct transliteration is claimed.

Master numbers and other systems

We preserve 11, 22 and 33 when reducing the total. Other traditions, including Chaldean numerology, use different letter values. We do not mix those systems. This calculator provides a single expression number, not separate soul-urge, personality or karmic readings.
