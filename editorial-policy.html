You should be able to tell which parts of a reading are arithmetic, which are interpretation and which claims are supported by an outside source. This page sets out those distinctions.

Who is responsible for this content?

Numberlune is the publishing identity responsible for these pages. Content and code were prepared with AI assistance. We do not present an invented individual author, professional credential or independent expert review. The published calculation rules, examples and limitations let you inspect the work directly.

Our three layers of content

  1. Arithmetic: deterministic code follows the conventions stated on the method page. The calculation is repeatable with the same input and rules.
  2. Symbolic interpretation: original editorial writing offers themes, scenarios and questions. These are not measured findings about an individual.
  3. External context: linked references support specific background points. They do not validate our number meanings or endorse Numberlune.

What we check

Automated calculation checks cover actual calendar dates, leap days, future birthdays, master numbers, the calendar-year convention and letter normalization. Each of the 12 published life path birthday examples is also checked against the calculator whenever the pages are generated.

Interface checks cover the four calculator flows, mobile layouts, error messages, keyboard tabs and exported cards. Checking that a formula runs as documented does not establish that numerology predicts anything.

How readings are written

Each number has a fixed core interpretation. We use possibility language and invite readers to look for counterexamples. Work scenarios and weekly exercises are suggestions that can be used by anyone; they are not personalized recommendations derived from a birthday.

We avoid lucky-number promises, guaranteed outcomes, fear-based labels, relationship success scores, diagnoses and rankings of human worth. A master number is a convention in the calculation, not a credential.

Sources and attribution

Our sources page explains the role of every external reference. The name “Pythagorean” describes the modern A–Z mapping used here; we do not claim that the ancient philosopher devised this English-letter calculator. No external author is credited as having reviewed our original readings.

Updates and corrections

November 6, 2026: published the transparent calculation conventions and expanded the number guides with checked examples, work scenarios and reflection exercises. This date records an actual content revision, not a promise of daily or expert review.

If a result looks wrong, first compare its displayed steps with the method. Check the entered date, the treatment of 11/22/33 and whether you are comparing a personal year with a life path. We do not offer private readings or a staffed support service, and no monitored contact channel is currently published.

Free access and commercial context

The calculators and guides are free. Ahrefs Web Analytics measures site usage as described in the privacy notice. Advertising spaces are reserved, but no advertising scripts are active in this release. Advertising does not determine a number or its interpretation.
