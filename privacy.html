Names and birthdays

The calculators process entries in your browser. This app does not submit names, birthdays, comparison inputs or reading results to a server, store them in cookies or local storage, or put them into page URLs. Reloading clears the form. The reading-card export intentionally omits names and birthdays. Copied readings include interpretation and calculation details, which may include a birthday’s numeric components or the normalized name. Review copied content before sharing it.

Ahrefs Web Analytics

All site HTML pages load Ahrefs Web Analytics using the site owner’s provided key to measure visits and usage. Ahrefs describes the service as cookie-free. Page URLs, referrers, browser/device details and connection information may be processed by the service. This app does not create analytics events containing your form entries or reading results. See Ahrefs privacy information for the provider’s practices. Third-party scripts run in the page; local calculation is not a guarantee about all third-party behavior.

Advertising

The calculator page loads a full-width HilltopAds banner at the top. It is part of the page layout and can be closed with its × button. The first click on a calculate, example, save-card or copy-reading button attempts to open an advertising page in a new tab, once per page load. The original tool action continues. Reading tabs, text fields, navigation links and the banner close button do not trigger this popup.

Advertising providers and destinations may process IP addresses, browser or device details, page visits and interaction information, and may use cookies, local storage or other identifiers. This app does not intentionally send names, birthdays, comparison inputs or reading results to advertising services. Third-party advertising scripts run in the calculator page and have their own data practices. See HilltopAds privacy information and the destination site’s policy.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may log requests, IP addresses and browser or connection information. External links are governed by the destination site’s policies. The referrer policy is set to no-referrer-when-downgrade as requested by the site owner; external requests may receive the referring page URL where that policy allows it. Calculator entries are not included in those URLs.

Your choices

You can explore a sample without entering personal details, close the banner with its × button, use browser controls or content blockers to manage advertising, popups and third-party analytics, and clear the page by reloading it. Downloads and copied content remain on your device until you remove them. Blocking analytics does not prevent the calculator from working.
