    LIFE PATH, PERSONAL YEAR & NAME NUMBERS

    One birthday. Three different questions.

    A life path number uses your full date of birth. A personal year uses your birth month and day with a selected calendar year. A name number uses the letters of a name. They describe different symbolic themes, so their results need not match.

    Numberlune keeps 11, 22 and 33 in life paths and name numbers. Personal years always reduce to 1–9. A matching description can be a useful prompt; it is not evidence of a prediction.

    Before you go.

    about the numbers.

    How we prepare our guides ↗
    What is a life path number?+

    In numerology, a life path number is a symbolic number calculated from your date of birth. Numberlune reduces the month, day and year separately, keeping 11, 22 and 33, then combines and reduces them. It is a reflection framework, not a scientific measure of personality.

    Why does another calculator give me a different number?+

    Numerology has no single standardized method. Adding every birth-date digit at once can produce a different master number from reducing the three date components separately. We show each step and explain our convention so you can compare like with like.

    Are 11, 22 and 33 better than other numbers?+

    No. Some traditions call these master numbers, but they are not a ranking, a promise or a burden. Their single-digit companions are 2, 4 and 6. A result of 33 appears only when our stated reduction method reaches it.

    When does a personal year start?+

    This calculator uses the calendar-year convention: January 1 through December 31. It reduces the birth month, birth day and selected year to a single digit from 1 to 9. Some traditions use a birthday-to-birthday cycle instead.

    Does a compatibility reading predict a relationship?+

    No. It compares symbolic themes from two life path numbers and offers conversation prompts. It assigns no success percentage and cannot determine whether a relationship will work.

    Is this free, and do you save my birthday or name?+

    All four tools and downloads are free. Calculations run in your browser. This app does not save names or birthdays, put them in URLs or intentionally send them to analytics. Ahrefs Web Analytics is enabled for site usage; see the privacy page for details.
