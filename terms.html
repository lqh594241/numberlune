Entertainment and reflection

Numerology is a symbolic tradition without established scientific validity as a way to assess personality or predict events. The calculators and interpretations are for entertainment and personal reflection. They are not medical, legal, financial, psychological or relationship advice.

Your judgment comes first

Do not use a reading as the sole basis for a consequential decision or as a reason to label, exclude or judge another person. Nothing on this site guarantees luck, a particular outcome or future events. No compatibility score or prediction of relationship success is provided.

Calculations and conventions

Results follow the published method. Numerology conventions differ, especially for master numbers and personal-year timing. We provide the arithmetic so you can understand a result. Browser support, mistakes in inputs and changes to source files can affect behavior.

Use of inputs and downloads

Enter information you are comfortable processing on your device. Use your own information, an example, or information you have permission to use. Downloaded reading cards are for personal use and sharing. Do not present the editorial interpretations as a professional diagnosis or personalized expert consultation.

Availability

The site is provided as available, without a guarantee of accuracy, uninterrupted availability, search ranking or any particular outcome. The tools do not require payment or registration in this version.
